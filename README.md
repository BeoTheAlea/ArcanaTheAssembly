# Arcana The Assembly

This is a project born out of a concern for the survival of a game losing its way with in by giving into the alure of the beyond in the year of 2026. A contigency plan with hopes of lasting beyond the life span of comercial goods and to give a lasting place for the game into culture at large. With all that comes with a game becoming a cultural concept, including the decentralized nature of iteration, concensus, and even disagreement. Hence hosting such an open mana project on GitHub. Where it can be forked, merged, and edited to the people's desires.

## Templating

This repository specificaly is to serve as a demonstration and template for making forks of Arcana the Assembly. Emphasis on the expactation that individual contributors may contribute to other forks, whether as by themselves or as part of a group, over contibuting to this base template.
